## Content / 内容

**Backpropagation** is reverse-mode automatic differentiation on the computational graph. For each node $v$ one computes the adjoint $\bar{v}=\partial L/\partial v$ by the chain rule, starting from $\bar{L}=1$ and proceeding in reverse topological order.  
For a linear layer $\mathbf{z}=W\mathbf{h}$ the adjoints are
$$
\bar{W}=\bar{\mathbf{z}}\mathbf{h}^\top,
\qquad
\bar{\mathbf{h}}=W^\top\bar{\mathbf{z}}.
$$
For an element-wise non-linearity the adjoint is simply multiplied by the local derivative $\phi'(z_i)$. The algorithm’s cost is a small constant factor times the cost of the forward pass, independent of the number of parameters.

反向传播 = 计算图上的反向模式自动微分。线性层与逐元素非线性的伴随公式是所有现代框架的核心。

The matrix-calculus identities $\nabla(W\mathbf{h})=\dots$ are exactly the local rules used by backprop.

## Practice / 练习

For the scalar function $L=\frac12(\sigma(wx)-y)^2$ write the three adjoint steps that recover $\partial L/\partial w$.

<details>
<summary>Solution sketch</summary>

$\bar{L}=1$, $\bar{\sigma}=(\sigma-y)$, $\bar{z}=\bar{\sigma}\cdot\sigma(1-\sigma)$, $\bar{w}=\bar{z}\cdot x$.

</details>
