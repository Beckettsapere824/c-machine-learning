## Content / 内容

A fully grown tree can achieve zero training error yet generalise poorly. Two remedies:
- **Pre-pruning**: stop splitting early (max depth, min samples, min impurity decrease);
- **Post-pruning**: grow fully then remove subtrees that do not improve validation performance (cost-complexity pruning).

Even pruned trees remain high-variance. Bagging them produces Random Forests; boosting them produces Gradient Boosted Trees—both among the strongest off-the-shelf classifiers.

预剪枝与后剪枝防止过拟合。进一步用 bagging/boosting 把多棵树集成为随机森林或提升树，显著降低方差。

## Practice / 练习

Why does increasing `max_depth` first decrease then increase test error?

<details>
<summary>Solution sketch</summary>

Bias falls while variance rises; the U-shaped curve is the classic bias-variance trade-off.

</details>
