# M0.12 Inequality Constraints & KKT Conditions (preview)

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M0.11 Lagrange Multipliers](M0.11.md)

---

## Content / 内容

When inequality constraints $g_i(\mathbf{w})\le0$ are present, the Lagrangian becomes
$$
\mathcal{L}(\mathbf{w},\boldsymbol{\alpha},\boldsymbol{\beta})=f(\mathbf{w})+\sum_i\alpha_i g_i(\mathbf{w})+\sum_j\beta_j h_j(\mathbf{w}),
$$
with multipliers $\alpha_i\ge0$. The **Karush–Kuhn–Tucker (KKT)** conditions at a local minimum are:

1. Stationarity: $\nabla_w\mathcal{L}=0$
2. Primal feasibility: $g_i(\mathbf{w})\le0$, $h_j(\mathbf{w})=0$
3. Dual feasibility: $\alpha_i\ge0$
4. Complementary slackness: $\alpha_i g_i(\mathbf{w})=0$ for every $i$

Complementary slackness is the key geometric fact: either a constraint is inactive ($g_i<0$) and its multiplier is zero, or the multiplier may be positive and the constraint is tight.

不等式约束引入非负乘子与互补松弛。软间隔 SVM 的对偶变量恰好落在区间 $[0,C]$，正是 KKT 互补松弛的体现。

**Why this matters later**  
In soft-margin SVM the box constraints $0\le\alpha_i\le C$ and the fact that support vectors lie on the margin (or inside the margin for non-zero slack) are direct consequences of the KKT complementary-slackness conditions.

## Example / 例子

Minimise $x$ subject to $x\ge0$.  
At the optimum $x^*=0$ the multiplier $\alpha^*=1>0$ and complementary slackness holds: $\alpha^*x^*=0$.

## Practice / 练习

Write the KKT system for $\min\frac12 w^2$ s.t. $w\ge1$.

<details>
<summary>Solution sketch</summary>

$\mathcal{L}=\frac12 w^2+\alpha(1-w)$, $\alpha\ge0$.  
Stationarity: $w-\alpha=0$.  
Complementary slackness: $\alpha(1-w)=0$.  
Feasibility: $w\ge1$.  
Solution $w=1$, $\alpha=1$.

</details>
