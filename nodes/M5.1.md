## Content / 内容

$k$-means partitions $n$ points into $k$ clusters $C_1,\dots,C_k$ by minimising the within-cluster sum of squares
$$
J = \sum_{c=1}^k\sum_{i\in C_c}\|\mathbf{x}_i-\boldsymbol{\mu}_c\|_2^2,
\qquad\boldsymbol{\mu}_c=\frac1{|C_c|}\sum_{i\in C_c}\mathbf{x}_i.
$$
Lloyd’s algorithm alternates two steps:
1. **Assignment**: each point is given to the nearest centroid;
2. **Update**: each centroid is replaced by the mean of its assigned points.

Both steps monotonically decrease $J$; the algorithm therefore converges to a local minimum after a finite number of iterations. Different random initialisations can (and often do) produce different local minima.

$k$-means 最小化簇内平方和。Lloyd 算法在“分配到最近中心”与“更新中心为均值”之间交替，单调下降直至局部最优。

## Tiny Python sketch
```python
from sklearn.cluster import KMeans
kmeans = KMeans(n_clusters=3, n_init=10).fit(X)
print(kmeans.inertia_)   # final value of J
```

## Practice / 练习

Show that for a fixed partition the optimal centroid is the mean of the points in the cluster.
