# M5.2 k-Means++ Initialization & Practical Issues

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M5.1 k-Means](M5.1.md)

---

## Content / 内容

Random initial centroids frequently lead to poor local minima. **$k$-means++** samples the first centroid uniformly, then samples each subsequent centroid with probability proportional to the squared distance to the nearest already-chosen centroid ($D^2$ sampling). The expected cost of the resulting initialisation is $O(\log k)$ times the optimal cost, giving a provable approximation guarantee.

Other practical concerns:
- empty clusters (re-initialise or split the largest cluster);
- choice of $k$ (elbow of the inertia curve, silhouette score—see M5.4);
- scaling of features (because Euclidean distance is used).

$k$-means++ 用 $D^2$ 抽样初始化，期望代价在最优解的 $O(\log k)$ 倍以内。空簇、特征尺度、$k$ 的选择都是实际中必须处理的问题。

## Tiny Python sketch

KMeans(n_clusters=5, init='k-means++', n_init=10)

## Practice / 练习

Why does feature standardisation usually improve $k$-means results?

<details>
<summary>Solution sketch</summary>

Euclidean distance treats every coordinate equally; variables with larger dynamic range otherwise dominate the assignment step.

</details>
