# M5.4 Cluster Validity Indices

## Content / 内容

When ground-truth labels are unavailable one relies on **internal** indices:
- **Silhouette** of a point: $(b-a)/\max(a,b)$ where $a$ is mean intra-cluster distance and $b$ is mean distance to the nearest other cluster; average silhouette close to 1 indicates good separation;
- **Davies–Bouldin**: average ratio of within-cluster scatter to between-centroid distance (lower is better).

When labels are known one uses **external** indices such as Adjusted Rand Index (ARI) or Normalised Mutual Information (NMI).

无标签时用轮廓系数、Davies–Bouldin 等内部指标；有标签时用 ARI、NMI 等外部指标。

## Tiny Python sketch
```python
from sklearn.metrics import silhouette_score
print(silhouette_score(X, labels))
```

## Practice / 练习

Why can the silhouette score be negative? What does a negative value mean for a particular point?

<details>
<summary>Solution sketch</summary>

$a>b$ means the point is on average closer to another cluster than to its own → it is probably mis-assigned.

</details>
