# M4.5 LDA – Generalized Eigenvalue Problem

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M4.4 Fisher Criterion](M4.4.md)

---

## Content / 内容

The critical points of the Rayleigh quotient $J(\mathbf{u})$ satisfy the **generalised eigenvalue equation**
$$
S_B\mathbf{u}=\lambda S_W\mathbf{u}.
$$
When $S_W$ is invertible this is equivalent to the ordinary eigenproblem
$$
S_W^{-1}S_B\mathbf{u}=\lambda\mathbf{u}.
$$
For a $C$-class problem there are at most $C-1$ non-zero generalised eigenvalues; the corresponding eigenvectors form the LDA projection matrix of size $d\times(C-1)$.  
In the rank-deficient case (common when $d>n$) one first projects onto the PCA subspace that makes $S_W$ full-rank, then solves the generalised eigenproblem—the classical “PCA + LDA” cascade used in Fisherfaces.

广义特征值方程 $S_B\mathbf{u}=\lambda S_W\mathbf{u}$。$C$ 类最多有 $C-1$ 个有用投影方向。维度高于样本数时先 PCA 再 LDA。

from sklearn.discriminant_analysis import LinearDiscriminantAnalysis
lda = LinearDiscriminantAnalysis(n_components=C-1)

## Practice / 练习

Why can the LDA subspace have dimension at most $C-1$?

<details>
<summary>Solution sketch</summary>

$S_B$ is a sum of $C$ rank-1 matrices that all live in the $(C-1)$-dimensional subspace orthogonal to the all-ones vector (because the weighted mean of the $\boldsymbol{\mu}_c$ is the global mean). Hence $\mathrm{rank}(S_B)\le C-1$.

</details>
