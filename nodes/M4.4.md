## Content / 内容

Linear Discriminant Analysis seeks a direction $\mathbf{u}$ that maximises the **Fisher criterion**
$$
J(\mathbf{u})=\frac{\mathbf{u}^\top S_B\mathbf{u}}{\mathbf{u}^\top S_W\mathbf{u}},
$$
where the **between-class scatter** $S_B$ and **within-class scatter** $S_W$ are
$$
S_B=\sum_c n_c(\boldsymbol{\mu}_c-\boldsymbol{\mu})(\boldsymbol{\mu}_c-\boldsymbol{\mu})^\top,
\qquad
S_W=\sum_c\sum_{i\in c}(\mathbf{x}_i-\boldsymbol{\mu}_c)(\mathbf{x}_i-\boldsymbol{\mu}_c)^\top.
$$
$J(\mathbf{u})$ is the ratio of projected between-class variance to projected within-class variance. Maximising it pushes class means apart while keeping each class tightly clustered.

Fisher 准则 = 类间散度 / 类内散度。LDA 找使该比值最大的投影方向，目标是“类间远、类内紧”。

## Practice / 练习

In a two-class problem with equal class covariances, show that the Fisher direction is parallel to $\Sigma^{-1}(\boldsymbol{\mu}_1-\boldsymbol{\mu}_2)$.

<details>
<summary>Solution sketch</summary>

$S_W\propto\Sigma$, $S_B\propto(\boldsymbol{\mu}_1-\boldsymbol{\mu}_2)(\boldsymbol{\mu}_1-\boldsymbol{\mu}_2)^\top$. The generalised eigenproblem reduces to the ordinary eigenproblem $\Sigma^{-1}S_B\mathbf{u}=\lambda\mathbf{u}$, whose only non-zero eigenvector is proportional to $\Sigma^{-1}(\boldsymbol{\mu}_1-\boldsymbol{\mu}_2)$.

</details>
