# M6.1 Majority / Plurality Voting & Weighted Voting

**Prerequisites / 前置**: none

---

## Content / 内容

Given $m$ base classifiers $C_1,\dots,C_m$, the **majority vote** (binary) or **plurality vote** (multi-class) predicts
$$
\hat{y}=\mathrm{mode}\{C_1(\mathbf{x}),\dots,C_m(\mathbf{x})\}.
$$
When the classifiers also output probability vectors $\mathbf{p}_j$, a soft vote averages them:
$$
\hat{y}=\arg\max_c\sum_{j=1}^m w_j p_{j,c}.
$$
Equal weights recover simple averaging; learned or validation-based weights can further improve performance.

多数/相对多数投票。软投票对概率加权平均。权重可相等，也可按验证集表现学习。

## Practice / 练习

Three classifiers predict probabilities for class 1 of 0.9, 0.8 and 0.4. With equal weights, what is the soft-vote probability? With weights (0.2,0.2,0.6)?

<details>
<summary>Solution sketch</summary>

Equal: $(0.9+0.8+0.4)/3=0.7$.  
Weighted: $0.2\cdot0.9+0.2\cdot0.8+0.6\cdot0.4=0.58$.

</details>
