# M0.11 Equality-Constrained Optimisation & Lagrange Multipliers

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M0.10 Optimality Condition](M0.10.md)

---

## Content / 内容

Consider the equality-constrained problem
$$
\min_{\mathbf{w}} f(\mathbf{w})\qquad\text{subject to}\quad h_i(\mathbf{w})=0,\quad i=1,\dots,\ell.
$$
The **Lagrangian** is the scalar function
$$
\mathcal{L}(\mathbf{w},\boldsymbol{\beta})=f(\mathbf{w})+\sum_{i=1}^\ell\beta_i h_i(\mathbf{w}),
$$
where the $\beta_i$ are called Lagrange multipliers.  
A necessary condition for a constrained local minimum (under a constraint-qualification) is the existence of multipliers such that
$$
\nabla_{\mathbf{w}}\mathcal{L}=\mathbf{0},\qquad
\nabla_{\boldsymbol{\beta}}\mathcal{L}=\mathbf{0}
$$
(the second block simply recovers the original constraints). Geometrically the gradient of the objective must be a linear combination of the gradients of the active constraints.

拉格朗日函数把约束问题变成无约束的驻点条件。硬间隔 SVM 的对偶推导正是从这里开始的。

**Why this matters later**  
The entire derivation of the hard-margin SVM dual consists of writing the Lagrangian of the primal, setting $\nabla_w\mathcal{L}=0$ and $\partial\mathcal{L}/\partial b=0$, and substituting back.

## Example / 例子

Minimise $f(x,y)=x^2+y^2$ subject to $x+y-1=0$.  
$\mathcal{L}=x^2+y^2+\beta(x+y-1)$.  
$\partial\mathcal{L}/\partial x=2x+\beta=0$, $\partial\mathcal{L}/\partial y=2y+\beta=0$, $x+y=1$.  
Solution $x=y=1/2$, $\beta=-1$.

## Practice / 练习

Derive the stationarity condition for $\min\frac12\|\mathbf{w}\|_2^2$ s.t. $\mathbf{w}^\top\mathbf{a}=1$.

<details>
<summary>Solution sketch</summary>

$\mathcal{L}=\frac12\|\mathbf{w}\|_2^2+\beta(1-\mathbf{w}^\top\mathbf{a})$.  
$\nabla_w\mathcal{L}=\mathbf{w}-\beta\mathbf{a}=0\Rightarrow\mathbf{w}=\beta\mathbf{a}$.  
Plug into the constraint: $\beta\|\mathbf{a}\|_2^2=1\Rightarrow\beta=1/\|\mathbf{a}\|_2^2$, $\mathbf{w}=\mathbf{a}/\|\mathbf{a}\|_2^2$.

</details>
