# M0.6 Positive (Semi)Definite Matrices & Quadratic Forms

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M0.5 Eigenvalues](M0.5.md)

---

## Content / 内容

A symmetric matrix $A$ is **positive semi-definite** (PSD), written $A\succeq0$, when
$$
\mathbf{x}^\top A\mathbf{x}\ge0\quad\forall\mathbf{x}.
$$
It is **positive definite** (PD) when the inequality is strict for $\mathbf{x}\ne0$.  
Equivalent characterisations:
- all eigenvalues $\ge0$ (respectively $>0$);
- there exists $B$ such that $A=B^\top B$ (Cholesky / square-root factorisation).

The quadratic form $\mathbf{x}^\top A\mathbf{x}$ is a convex function precisely when $A\succeq0$. Consequently any optimisation problem whose Hessian is PSD is convex and every local minimum is global.

二次型 $\mathbf{x}^\top A\mathbf{x}$ 非负当且仅当 $A$ 半正定。半正定 Hessian 保证凸性，从而没有糟糕的局部极小——这是最小二乘和 SVM 对偶有唯一解的根本原因。

**Why this matters later**  
- The covariance matrix is always PSD.  
- The Hessian of the least-squares objective is $X^\top X\succeq0$.  
- The dual of hard-margin SVM is a concave quadratic maximisation over a PSD Gram matrix.

## Example / 例子

$$
A=\begin{pmatrix}2&1\\1&2\end{pmatrix}\succeq0
$$
because eigenvalues are $3$ and $1$. The quadratic form $2x^2+2xy+2y^2=(x+y)^2+x^2+y^2\ge0$.

## Tiny Python sketch
```python
A = np.array([[2.,1.],[1.,2.]])
print(np.linalg.eigvalsh(A))   # all ≥0 → PSD
```

## Practice / 练习

Prove that if $A\succeq0$ and $B$ is any matrix of compatible size, then $B^\top A B\succeq0$.

<details>
<summary>Solution sketch</summary>

$\mathbf{y}^\top(B^\top A B)\mathbf{y}=(B\mathbf{y})^\top A(B\mathbf{y})\ge0$ by the PSD property of $A$.

</details>
