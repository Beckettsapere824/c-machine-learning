## Content / 内容

Practical PCA pipeline:
1. Centre the data.
2. Compute $\Sigma$ (or, for $n\ll d$, work with the smaller Gram matrix $\tilde{X}\tilde{X}^\top$ and recover eigenvectors via a dual relation).
3. Take the top-$k$ eigenvectors $Q_k$.
4. Project: $\mathbf{z}=Q_k^\top(\mathbf{x}-\boldsymbol{\mu})$.

**Whitening** further rescales each coordinate by $1/\sqrt{\lambda_i}$ so that the resulting covariance is the identity.  
In face recognition the leading eigenvectors of the pixel covariance, reshaped back into image dimensions, are the classic **eigenfaces**. A new face is projected onto the eigenface basis and then classified by $k$-NN (or any other classifier) in the low-dimensional coefficient space—precisely the pipeline motivated in M1.3.

实际步骤：中心化 → 特征分解 → 取前 $k$ 个主成分投影。白化额外除以 $\sqrt{\lambda_i}$。Eigenfaces 就是把特征向量还原成图像形状。
