# M7.2 Gini Impurity

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M7.1 Entropy](M7.1.md)

---

## Content / 内容

The **Gini impurity** of a distribution is
$$
G(\mathbf{p})=1-\sum_k p_k^2.
$$
It is zero for a pure node and reaches its maximum $1-1/K$ for the uniform distribution. Gini is faster to compute than entropy (no logarithms) and yields very similar trees in practice; CART uses Gini by default while C4.5/ID3 use entropy.

Gini 不纯度 $1-\sum p_k^2$。计算比熵更快，产生的树与信息增益几乎相同。

## Practice / 练习

Compare Gini and entropy for a binary problem with $p=0.5$ and with $p=0.9$.

<details>
<summary>Solution sketch</summary>

$p=0.5$: Gini $=0.5$, entropy $=1$.  
$p=0.9$: Gini $=0.18$, entropy $\approx0.469$. Both decrease as the distribution becomes purer.

</details>
