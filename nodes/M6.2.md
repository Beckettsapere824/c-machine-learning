# M6.2 Bagging (Bootstrap Aggregating)

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M6.1 Voting](M6.1.md)

---

## Content / 内容

Bagging draws $m$ bootstrap samples (sampling with replacement) of the training set, trains a base learner on each, and aggregates their predictions by voting or averaging.  
Because the bootstrap samples are only partially correlated, the variance of the averaged predictor is reduced while bias stays roughly the same. The method is especially effective for high-variance base learners such as deep decision trees (Random Forests are bagged trees with an extra random-feature twist).

Bagging 对自助采样集并行训练基学习器再聚合。主要降低方差，对高方差模型（深树）特别有效。

## Tiny Python sketch
```python
from sklearn.ensemble import BaggingClassifier
bag = BaggingClassifier(base_estimator=DecisionTreeClassifier(), n_estimators=50)
```

## Practice / 练习

Why does bagging rarely help a $k$-NN classifier with large $k$?

<details>
<summary>Solution sketch</summary>

Large-$k$ nearest-neighbour is already a low-variance, high-bias estimator; averaging many similar low-variance models yields little further reduction.

</details>
