## 2.1 Counting Rules

**Theorem 2.1 (Product Rule)**  
Procedure = sequence of two tasks: \(n_1\) ways for first, \(n_2\) for second → \(n_1 n_2\) ways total.

**Corollary 2.1 (Multiplication Principle)**  
\(k\) steps with \(n_i\) ways each → \(\prod_{i=1}^k n_i\) ways.

Examples: 3 coloured dice → 6³ = 216 ordered outcomes; seating 4 people in a row → 4! = 24; license plates (2 letters + 4 digits) → 26² × 10⁴ = 6 760 000.

**Theorem 2.2 (Sum Rule)**  
Task can be done in \(n_1\) or \(n_2\) *disjoint* ways → \(n_1 + n_2\).

**Theorem 2.3 (Subtraction Rule)**  
\(n_1 + n_2\) minus the common ways (simple inclusion-exclusion).

**Theorem 2.4 (Division Rule)**  
If a procedure can be carried out in \(n\) ways and every distinct outcome corresponds to exactly \(d\) of those ways, there are \(n/d\) distinct outcomes.  
(Circular seating of 4 people → 4!/4 = 6.)
